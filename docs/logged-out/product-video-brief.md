# Product Video: Creative Brief

**Owner:** Bradley
**Status:** Planning — not yet in production
**Last updated:** 2026-03-18

---

## Concept Summary

A 60–90 second animated product overview video. No live footage. Visual style: **cartoon / illustrated people** — warm, slightly stylised, not photorealistic. Think Notion's early explainer style or Linear's illustration palette, but with more personality.

The video moves through three real-world Axel moments — morning, midday, afternoon — then lands on the brand statement.

**End card:** *"Meet Axel. One assistant. Every kind of work."*

---

## Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

---

### Scene 1 — Morning Notification / Planner

**Duration:** ~20 seconds

**Setting:** A person's bedroom or kitchen. Morning light. Phone or laptop on a table. They look tired, coffee in hand.

**Action:**
- A soft notification appears on the device. Axel's interface.
- Axel delivers a brief: *"Good morning. You have 3 meetings today. Your 10am prep is ready. Two tasks from yesterday are still open."*
- The person's expression shifts — calm, oriented, ready.

**Voiceover (or on-screen text):**
> "Start every day knowing what matters."

**Visual cues:**
- Illustrated calendar strips, task chips, and a small avatar for Axel — subtle, not intrusive
- Warm tones: amber, cream, soft blues
- Axel should feel like a presence, not a notification spammer

**Transition:** The device screen brightens and zooms gently into the next scene.

---

### Scene 2 — Meeting Summary / Operator

**Duration:** ~20 seconds

**Setting:** A conference room or video call grid. Several illustrated people around a table or in a call. Slightly more corporate — but still warm.

**Action:**
- The meeting ends. Everyone else disperses.
- A summary appears in the Axel interface: action items, decisions, follow-ups assigned by name.
- The person at the centre (the "operator") nods, closes the laptop. Done.

**Voiceover (or on-screen text):**
> "Every meeting, captured. Every decision, remembered."

**Visual cues:**
- Meeting transcript condensing down into 3 clean bullet points — visual metaphor, not literal UI
- Illustrated name badges or avatars floating briefly to show "assigned to" moment
- Slightly cooler tones — professional, midday energy

**Transition:** Pull back from the conference room into a wider shot that morphs into a code editor.

---

### Scene 3 — Developer / Sub-Agent Review

**Duration:** ~20 seconds

**Setting:** A developer at a desk — multiple screens, a bit more technical vibe but still illustrated/cartoon.

**Action:**
- A sub-agent task grid is visible: multiple tasks delegated, in-progress, completed
- The developer reviews a summary — Axel has orchestrated several agents to complete a larger task
- They approve one, dismiss another, ship

**Voiceover (or on-screen text):**
> "For builders: orchestrate agents, review outputs, stay in control."

**Visual cues:**
- Task pipeline as a visual flow (nodes → arrows → outputs) — abstract but readable
- Code on screen doesn't need to be real code; abstract syntax blocks work
- Darker background on the screens, developer energy, but character design stays consistent

**Transition:** Zoom out to show all three scenes side by side briefly — morning, meeting, code — before the end card.

---

### End Card

**Duration:** ~10 seconds

**Visual:**
- All three characters from Scenes 1–3 appear in a simple three-panel layout or unified illustrated scene
- Axel's logo or wordmark appears
- Text fades in:

```
Meet Axel.
One assistant. Every kind of work.
```

- CTA: `axel.ai` (or whatever the live URL is) + "Start for free"
- Soft, confident music fade-out

## Visual Style Notes

- **Illustration style:** Cartoon people. Not clipart. Not photorealistic. Think: flat vector with subtle shadows, warm skin tones, expressive without being over-the-top. Consistent character design across all scenes.
- **Colour palette:** Warm neutrals + one accent colour (blue or amber — match product brand when confirmed)
- **Animation style:** Smooth but not overproduced. Slide-ins, fades, and gentle zooms. No jarring cuts.
- **Text on screen:** Used sparingly. Voiceover is preferred. If going text-only, keep it to one line per moment.
- **Axel's presence:** Axel shouldn't be anthropomorphised as a cartoon character himself unless the brand commits to that. Prefer: a clean interface, subtle indicator, or small abstract avatar.

---

## Audio

- **Music:** Light, modern, non-distracting. Instrumental. Avoid anything that sounds like a corporate explainer from 2016.
- **Voiceover:** Warm, confident, natural pace. Not "AI voice demo" energy. If using a real voice, brief it with the script and ask for a conversational read.
- **SFX:** Minimal. Soft notification sound on Scene 1. Nothing else needs to be called out.

## What This Video Is Not

- Not a feature walkthrough. Don't show every button.
- Not a technical deep-dive. The developer scene shows capability, not implementation.
- Not aspirational fluff. Every scene shows something Axel actually does today (or at launch).

## Placement

- **Primary:** Homepage above the fold or just below hero, autoplaying muted with captions
- **Secondary:** Use Cases page as supporting media
- **Optional:** LinkedIn / social cut (30s version, subtitled)
